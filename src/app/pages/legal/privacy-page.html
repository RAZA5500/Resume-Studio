Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

ResumeStudio (resumestudio.site and the ResumeStudio Android app) helps you build resumes, check them against applicant tracking systems (ATS) and edit documents. This page explains what information we collect, why we use it, who processes it for us and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, name and, if you add it, a headline. If you set a password we store only a secure hash of it, never the password itself. If you turn on two-factor authentication, its secret is stored encrypted and its backup codes only as hashes.
  • Sign in with Google or Apple: if you choose it, we receive your name, your email address, whether that address is verified and an account identifier from Google or Apple. We do not receive your Google or Apple password and get no access to your contacts, files, email or any other data in those accounts.
  • What you create: resumes, cover letters, ATS reports (including the job descriptions you paste) and the documents and files you upload or edit.
  • Payments: you pay in your own JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank app, or on the page of an online payment provider. We keep the plan, amount, method, transaction ID and, for manual payments, the sender number, sender name and receipt screenshot you give us, so that we can confirm the payment. We never see your card details, PINs or wallet passwords.
  • Technical information: your IP address is used for a short time to protect sign-in and to limit abuse; it is not stored with your account. We also count how many resumes, documents and AI requests you use each day to apply the limits of your plan.

How we use it

  • To run the service: your account, saving your work and exporting it.
  • To keep accounts safe: verification emails, two-factor authentication and protection against abuse.
  • To confirm payments and apply your plan's limits.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and ResumeStudio has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Who processes data for us

  • Hostinger hosts the website and the mailbox that sends our emails.
  • Supabase hosts our database (servers in Singapore).
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it sends the request to (for example Anthropic) receive the text you give an AI feature, such as a resume section or a job description, only when you use that feature. Your password and payment details are never sent to them.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Have I Been Pwned: when you choose a password, we send only the first five characters of its SHA-1 hash to check whether it has appeared in a known data breach. Your password itself never leaves our server.

These providers may process data outside Pakistan. They act on our behalf and may use it only to provide their service to us.

Information from Google

If you sign in with Google, we use only your basic profile (name, email address and account identifier) to create your account and sign you in. We do not share it with others, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. ResumeStudio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and storage on your device

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in; scripts on the page cannot read it, and it is sent only to our sign-in routes. Your browser's storage remembers your session and preferences on that device, such as the theme and sidebar. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Your work stays in your account until you delete it. You can delete resumes, documents and ATS reports in the app at any time.
  • You can change your name and sign-in settings under Profile.
  • To delete your whole account, contact us (see below) with the email address of the account; we may ask you to confirm that it is yours. We delete the account and its content within 30 days, except payment records we have to keep for accounting.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, two-factor secrets are encrypted and sign-in is protected against repeated guessing. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

Children

ResumeStudio is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
